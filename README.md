Privacy Policy for Wild Snake

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Wild Snake is a mobile game developed and published by Imralix Gaming Studio.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Wild Snake game.

1. Information We Collect

Wild Snake does not directly collect or request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, home address, passwords, contacts, photos or videos, precise location, or financial information.

The game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play.

2. Game Data

Wild Snake may store certain game settings and progress locally on your device, such as:

High score
Sound settings
Music settings
Vibration settings
Control preferences
This information is used to provide the game's features and improve your gameplay experience.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Wild Snake may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about ad interactions, depending on the applicable settings and services.

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google's advertising policies.

For more information about how Google uses information in advertising, please review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Device Permissions

Wild Snake does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for normal gameplay.

5. Third-Party Services

Wild Snake may use third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Wild Snake does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. Since Wild Snake does not directly collect personal information, there is no personal information stored by us through the game.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the game, third-party services, or applicable requirements. Any updated Privacy Policy will be made available on this page.
